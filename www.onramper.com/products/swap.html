Frequently asked questions.

What is Onramper Swap?

Onramper Swap is a premium swap engine powered by XOSwap Exodus is the parent company of XO Swap. Exodus is an industry-leading wallet established over 9 years ago. The Exodus team has a deep understanding of how to create premium user experiences that provide exceptional value and drive profitability. This allows anyone to integrate swap enginner which support 50 + networks.

How compliant is Exodus?

With 9 years of experience, Halborn audits, and an active bug bounty program, XO Swap is powered by premium security. XO Swap's parent company Exodus is regulated by the SEC and is audited by Deloitte on a monthly basis.

How is Onramper Swap better than building our own swap feature in-house?

Save engineering costs and increase speed to market. Benefit from our experience and expertise, there is no need for you to solve each obstacle we've already overcome. Expedite your integration by harnessing the Exodus Product and Engineering teams. With only four API calls, XO Swap can be quickly integrated and ready for testing within hours. You can rely on Exodus realtime support and advice.

How does Onramper Swap handle transaction fees and costs?

All fees are built into the quotes. The only additional fee that needs to be accommodated for is the pay transaction fee from your wallet or client.

Does Onramper also offer widget or just API?

Onramper offers a swap widget with built-in walletconnect for EVM connectivity.
